Ant Mill Simulation

Biological Swarm Active Ants flush amber under metabolic stress, collapsing if trapped in tight loops (die if loop for 10 iterations). Mouse click or touch to attract the ants.

Wander Chaos: Controls baseline randomness when ants aren't tracking a scent. Low values create straight exploration paths; high values lock them into erratic, dense clusters.

Sensor Distance: Sets how many pixels ahead left/center/right antennae sniff out trails. Short distances cause near-sightedness; long distances pull them into massive loops prematurely.

Player Trail Decay: Sets how fast interactive blue paths evaporate. Lower decay anchors steady highways; high decay forces you to draw continuously to direct the flow.
